Our Services

Single Tooth Implant

A Single Tooth Implant replaces both the root and crown of a missing tooth without impacting adjacent healthy teeth, offering a seamless, natural appearance and function.

Implant Supported Bridge

An Implant Supported Bridge is an ideal solution for replacing multiple consecutive missing teeth. It anchors to durable titanium implants rather than natural teeth for maximum stability.

Full Mouth Dental Implants

For patients facing extensive tooth loss, Full Mouth Dental Implants restore entire upper or lower arches, bringing back optimal chewing ability, structure, and aesthetic beauty.

Full Arch Dental Implants

Our Full Arch Dental Implants provide a permanent, non-removable alternative to traditional dentures, securely fixed using strategically placed dental implant posts.

2. What is the advantage of a Single Tooth Implant over a traditional dental bridge?

A single tooth implant replaces the root structure in your jawbone, which preserves bone height and prevents adjacent teeth from needing to be trimmed down, as is required for a traditional bridge.

3. How does an Implant Supported Bridge work?

An implant-supported bridge uses titanium implant posts anchored directly into the jawbone at either end of a tooth gap, securely holding a custom bridge in place without altering natural neighboring teeth.

4. Are Full Mouth Dental Implants better than traditional dentures?

Yes. Full mouth dental implants are fixed permanently into the jaw, preventing slipping, bone loss, and gum irritation associated with conventional removable dentures, allowing you to eat and speak normally.

5. How long is the recovery time for Full Arch Dental Implants?

Initial healing typically takes a few days, while full osseointegration (implant fusing with the jawbone) takes 3 to 6 months. Most patients return to light daily activities within 24–48 hours post-procedure.
